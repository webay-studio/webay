# STAY, SLOW. — brand website concept

Reviewed September 17, 2026. Original fictional hospitality brand, presented as the third design direction on the webay home page.

- STAYFOLIO: https://www.stayfolio.com/ — space discovery and a journal-like approach to travel. Applied to room browsing, capacity filters, and the sequence from brand story to choosing a stay.
- Amanemu: https://www.aman.com/resorts/amanemu — architecture, landscape, accommodation, and experiences presented as one hospitality story. Applied to large photographs and a separate daily-experience section.
- Aesop spaces: https://www.aesop.com/de/en/r/store-experience/ — contextual stories about physical spaces. The reference currently redirects to the regional storefront. Applied as an editorial direction with restrained colors and material-focused copy, without copying brand assets or page layouts.

Visual direction: warm ivory, muted terracotta, editorial serif headlines, asymmetric photographs, generous spacing, understated rules instead of rounded card containers.
